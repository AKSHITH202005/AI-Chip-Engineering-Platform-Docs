# Product Capability Map

## Purpose

This document defines the major capabilities of the AI Chip Engineering Platform (ACEP). Each capability represents a logical area of responsibility that will later be refined into features, use cases, and requirements.

---

# Engineering Lifecycle

## Requirements Engineering

Capture, analyze, refine, and manage hardware requirements.

---

## RTL Design

Assist engineers in creating, reviewing, and maintaining synthesizable RTL.

---

## Functional Verification

Generate verification environments, execute simulations, analyze failures, and improve coverage.

---

## Physical Design

Support synthesis, timing analysis, floorplanning, routing, and physical implementation workflows.

---

## Signoff

Assist with timing closure, DRC/LVS review, power analysis, and final design validation.

---

## Documentation

Generate and maintain engineering documentation throughout the project lifecycle.

---

# Platform Services

## AI Orchestration

Coordinate specialized AI agents.

## Engineering Knowledge Graph

Store and retrieve engineering knowledge.

## Workflow Engine

Execute engineering workflows.

## Project Management

Track artifacts, tasks, and project state.

## API Layer

Expose platform functionality through APIs.

## Authentication & Authorization

Manage user identity and permissions.
