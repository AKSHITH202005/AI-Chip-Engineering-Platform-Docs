# System Vision

## 1. Executive Summary

The AI Chip Engineering Platform (ACEP) is an AI-native engineering operating system for semiconductor design.

ACEP combines specialized AI agents, engineering knowledge, workflow orchestration, and EDA tool integration into a unified platform that assists engineers throughout the complete integrated circuit (IC) design lifecycle.

Rather than replacing engineers, ACEP augments engineering teams by automating repetitive tasks, providing intelligent recommendations, maintaining engineering knowledge, and orchestrating complex design workflows while keeping humans in control of all critical engineering decisions.

---

## 2. Why ACEP Exists

Modern semiconductor design is one of the most complex engineering disciplines in existence.

A single chip project may involve:

- Hundreds of engineers
- Thousands of design files
- Multiple EDA tools
- Numerous verification stages
- Large volumes of documentation
- Constant design iterations

Engineering knowledge is often fragmented across documentation, source code, emails, scripts, and experienced engineers.

ACEP exists to unify this knowledge into an intelligent engineering platform.

---

## 3. Vision Statement

To become the AI-native operating system for semiconductor engineering by enabling engineers to design faster, collaborate better, and make higher-quality engineering decisions through intelligent automation.

---

## 4. Mission Statement

Develop an extensible engineering platform that combines artificial intelligence, engineering knowledge, workflow orchestration, and industry-standard EDA tools into a unified development environment.

---

## 5. Product Definition

ACEP is an AI-native engineering operating system that orchestrates specialized AI agents, engineering knowledge, and EDA tools to assist the complete semiconductor design lifecycle while keeping engineers in control of every critical engineering decision.

---

## 6. Core Principles

- Human-in-the-loop engineering
- AI-assisted, not AI-replaced
- Knowledge-driven automation
- Modular architecture
- Tool interoperability
- Traceability
- Security by design

---

## 7. Long-Term Vision

ACEP aims to become the central engineering platform connecting requirements, RTL design, verification, physical design, documentation, project management, and engineering knowledge into a single intelligent workspace.

---

## 8. Success Criteria

Success will be measured by:

- Reduced engineering effort
- Faster design iterations
- Improved documentation quality
- Better knowledge retention
- Increased automation
- Higher design quality
- Seamless EDA integration
